# AI Assistant for Instagram Content Strategy

## Summary
An AI assistant that helps SMM managers and small businesses create Instagram content ideas, captions, and strategies using artificial intelligence.

## Background
Many small business owners and SMM managers struggle to create consistent content for Instagram.

## How is it used?
The user enters their niche and audience, and AI suggests content ideas.

## Data sources and AI methods
Uses trends, public data, and NLP.

## Challenges
Generic content and privacy issues.

## What next?
